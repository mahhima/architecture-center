Event-driven integration is a foundational pattern in modern enterprise architectures. SAP Integration Suite, combined with SAP Event Mesh, makes it straightforward to decouple producers and consumers across S/4HANA and line-of-business applications.

<!-- truncate -->

The key benefit is resilience — a failed consumer does not block the producer, and events can be replayed. For teams starting out, a simple publish-subscribe topology is enough to get going.
